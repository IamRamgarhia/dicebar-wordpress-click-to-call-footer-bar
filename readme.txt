A bottom bar on phones and tablets holding whatever you put in it.

== Description ==

Footer Bar puts a fixed bar across the bottom of the screen on phones and tablets,
holding whatever you choose to put in it. A phone number, a message link, an
announcement, a social profile, or a link to anywhere on your site.

Every item is added, ordered and configured from the settings screen. Nothing is
hard coded and nothing is fetched from anywhere else. Footer Bar makes no external
network requests and collects no analytics of any kind.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin to your plugins directory, or install it from the plugin
   directory inside your dashboard.
2. Activate it.
3. Open Settings, then Footer Bar, and add your items.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it work with page caching? =

Yes. Rules that depend on the address of the page are worked out on the server
and cache correctly. Rules that change within one address, such as opening hours
and whether the visitor is signed in, are worked out in the browser.
